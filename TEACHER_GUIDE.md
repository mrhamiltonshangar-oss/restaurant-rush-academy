# Teacher Guide: Restaurant Rush Academy

## Quick setup

1. Sign in with your school email.
2. Create a class for the term.
3. Add sections such as `Period 2 Culinary` or `Period 5 FCS Lab`.
4. Share the generated class code with students.
5. Turn on or off:
   - team mode
   - leaderboards
   - simplified instructions
   - larger text defaults
   - unit unlocks

## Recommended semester pacing

1. Weeks 1-2: `Kitchen Orientation`, `Opening Day`
2. Weeks 3-5: `Breakfast Rush`, `Baking Pop-Up`, `Dairy and Egg Challenge`
3. Weeks 6-8: `Fruits and Vegetables Prep Sprint`, `Meat and Poultry Safety Showdown`
4. Weeks 9-10: `Customer Complaint Recovery`, `Equipment Breakdown Chaos`
5. Weeks 11-12: `Allergy-Safe Catering`, `Team Service Showdown`
6. Final week: `Final Restaurant Championship`

## What to watch on the dashboard

- Completion status for each student or team
- Time played to spot disengagement or over-struggle
- Sanitation flags to target reteaching
- Station-level stuck points
- Reflection quality and standards progress
- Fun/engagement patterns using replay frequency, retries, and achievement unlocks

## Suggested grading approach

- Completion: 50%
- Safety and sanitation: 25%
- Reflection responses: 15%
- Challenge performance: 10%

Keep tutorial and guided mode low-stakes. Use challenge mode only after students have had time to learn first.

## Resetting progress

Use reset only when a student is genuinely blocked or restarting a partnered team run. Resetting should preserve archival logs whenever possible so you still retain evidence of participation.

## Archiving a term

1. Archive the class after the semester ends.
2. Duplicate the class into a new term.
3. Keep old sections archived rather than deleting them.
4. Review archived sanitation and standards trends before planning the next term.
